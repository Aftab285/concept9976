A Story That Needs to Be Told

For too many veterans, missing dental care becomes a daily barrier: pain that goes untreated, interviews avoided, meals skipped, and confidence quietly worn down.

This is the story at the heart of the Veterans Dental Foundation: real people, real obstacles, and the life-changing power of a restored smile.

The Coverage Gap

Dental care should never be the missing promise.

Most veterans do not qualify for comprehensive VA dental benefits. That leaves essential care out of reach until pain, infection, or visible damage forces a crisis.

Benefits are limited

Eligibility rules leave many veterans without routine cleanings, restorations, dentures, or urgent oral surgery support.

Costs delay care

Out-of-pocket treatment can cost thousands, forcing veterans to choose between dental health and essentials.

Oral health affects life

Dental disease can affect nutrition, employment, heart health, sleep, and the ability to show up confidently.
